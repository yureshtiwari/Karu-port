About Me

My journey from customer service to design & development

I started my career in customer service where I learned how to listen, synthesize feedback, and design experiences that remove friction. This foundation taught me that great products are built on empathy and understanding real user needs.

I studied Computer Science and transitioned into front-end development and UI/UX design — building interfaces with React, crafting intuitive user flows, and using Tableau and CRM systems to turn data into product decisions.

I specialize in translating customer needs into practical interfaces that are accessible, fast, and human-centered.
