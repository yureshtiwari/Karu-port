Background & Philosophy

I started my career in customer service where I learned how to listen, synthesize feedback, and design experiences that remove friction. I studied Computer Science and transitioned into front-end development and UI/UX design — building interfaces with React, crafting user flows, and using Tableau and CRM systems to turn data into product decisions.

I specialize in translating customer needs into practical interfaces that are accessible, fast, and human-centered.
